# Privacy policy

Life YouTube is a personal tool used only by its author, Brandon Kraeling, on his own Google account.

- **Data accessed:** the YouTube account's playlists, and video titles and lengths, used only to add videos to a private playlist the tool created.
- **Storage:** the sign-in token is stored only on the author's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.
- **Sharing:** no data is collected from anyone else, sold, or shared.
- **Revoking access:** go to [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Contact: open an issue at [github.com/bkraeling/life-youtube](https://github.com/bkraeling/life-youtube/issues).
